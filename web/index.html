How to try it

  1. Press Start camera and allow camera access.
  2. Hold your left hand in the left half and move it to the edges to press a direction. Point with the index finger for an R2 tap.
  3. Show your right hand in the right half as a palm, thumbs up, peace sign, or L shape. Make a fist to release.

Keep your palm facing the camera, about 30–50 cm away, in even lighting.

Runs entirely on your device with MediaPipe Hand Landmarker. The camera stream never leaves the browser.
