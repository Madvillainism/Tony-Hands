02 How to ride

  1. 1

    Steer with the left hand

    Left of the frame midline steers left; right of it steers right. The top and bottom thirds handle up and down, and both axes work together for diagonals.

  2. 2

    Trick with the right hand

    Open palm is Cross, thumbs up is Circle, peace is Triangle, an L is Square. Point with the left hand for an R2 tap. Thumbs down pauses the game.

  3. 3

    Make a fist to let go

    Any unrecognised shape releases the held button. Moving your hand out of frame releases movement too, so nothing sticks.

Trick sheet

Hands

  • ✋ Open palm Cross Crouch and hold. Make a fist to ollie.
  • 👍 Thumbs up Circle Grab.
  • 👎 Thumbs down Pause Upright fist, thumb down. Repeat to resume.
  • ✌️ Peace sign Triangle Grind.
  • 🤘 L shape Square Flip trick.
  • ☝️ Point, left hand R2 tap 120 ms press, 250 ms cooldown.
  • ✊ Fist Release Drops whatever is held.

Zones

The left half of the frame is a 3×3 pad. Left of the midline is left, right of it is right — there is no neutral band. The top third is up and the bottom third is down, with a 5% hysteresis band so the edge of a vertical zone cannot flicker.

Both axes work at once, so diagonals are possible.

Ride with your hands

  • ✋ Cross

    Open palm holds Cross. Make a fist to ollie.

  • ☝️ D-Pad

    Left of the frame steers left; right of center steers right.

  • ✌️ Triangle

    Peace grinds. Thumbs up grabs, an L flips. Thumbs down pauses.

  • ✊ Release

    A fist lets go of whatever you are holding.

  • 🖐️ Neutral

    Move a hand out of frame and its inputs release too.

Remap keys

Click a key, then press a new one. Escape cancels. Changes apply immediately and are saved for next time.
