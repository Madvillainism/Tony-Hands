02 How to ride

  1. 1

    Steer with the left hand

    Hold it in the left half of the frame and move to an edge for a direction. Diagonals work: the axes are independent.

  2. 2

    Trick with the right hand

    Open palm is Cross, thumbs up is Circle, peace is Triangle, an L is Square. Point with the left hand for an R2 tap.

  3. 3

    Make a fist to let go

    Any unrecognised shape releases the held button. Moving your hand out of frame releases movement too, so nothing sticks.

Trick sheet

Gestures

  • ✋ Open palm Cross Crouch and hold. Make a fist to ollie.
  • 👍 Thumbs up Circle Grab.
  • ✌️ Peace sign Triangle Grind.
  • 🤘 L shape Square Flip trick.
  • ☝️ Point, left hand R2 tap 120 ms press, 250 ms cooldown.
  • ✊ Fist Release Drops whatever is held.

Zones

The left half of the frame is a 3×3 pad: 20% from the left edge is left, 30% from the right edge is right, the top quarter is up and the bottom quarter is down. Each zone has a 5% hysteresis band so the edge of a zone cannot flicker.

Move deliberately between zones to avoid accidental turns.

Ride with your hands

  • ✋ Cross

    Open palm holds Cross. Make a fist to ollie.

  • ☝️ D-Pad

    Left palm moves to an edge of the zone grid to steer.

  • ✌️ Triangle

    Peace grinds. Thumbs up grabs, an L flips.

  • ✊ Release

    A fist lets go of whatever you are holding.

  • 🖐️ Neutral

    Move a hand out of frame and its inputs release too.
